Keycloak is up in {{ .Release.Namespace }} (infra-only — nothing in Profolio consumes it yet).

Check it:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods,svc

Reach the admin console:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-keycloakx 8080:80

Then open http://localhost:8080 and sign in with the bootstrap admin credentials from the
keycloak-admin-credentials Secret.
